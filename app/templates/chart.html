Understanding Your Results
Key Metrics Explained:
  • Resting Blood Pressure: Normal range is typically below 120/80 mm Hg.
  • Serum Cholesterol: Desirable levels are below 200 mg/dL.
  • Maximum Heart Rate: Varies by age, but a higher maximum heart rate during exercise is generally associated with better cardiovascular fitness.
  • ST Depression: ST depression induced by exercise is an indicator used to evaluate heart function during stress tests.
What These Charts Tell You:
  • Comparing your current metrics with previous readings helps track changes over time.
  • Consistent improvements in these metrics often indicate improved heart health.
  • Sudden or significant changes may warrant discussion with your healthcare provider.
  • These visualizations are meant to complement, not replace, professional medical advice.
